About Flora

A quiet study of form, colour and the architecture of petals.

The idea

Flora is a digital herbarium. Each species is rebuilt petal by petal in real time, so you can turn it in your hands, watch it open and study the geometry nature took millions of years to refine.

The family

Every flower here belongs to Asteraceae — the daisy family. What looks like a single bloom is in fact a composite head of hundreds of tiny florets working as one.

The craft

Procedural 3D petals, physically based light and cinematic transitions. No two petals are identical; each is shaped, cupped and coloured on its own.
